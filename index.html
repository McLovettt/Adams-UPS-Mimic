Training simulator only. This follows the switching logic you described and is not a substitute for an approved manufacturer/site switching procedure. Battery/DC energy is shown for training only. The battery percentage drains/recharges at an intentionally accelerated demo rate. The 5-second test simulates a rectifier failure with a healthy bypass source: when the battery reaches 0%, the UPS enters FORCED BYPASS if the bypass source is available and synchronised. If mains/bypass is unavailable, the simulator reports LOAD DROPPED!! and BYPASS FAILED. The mechanical maintenance-bypass breaker (MBB) remains manual. In this training model the UPS output and bypass/load bus are treated as out of sync until Static Bypass is selected. In Castell mode, locking the key in Position 2 releases the MBB; the MBB must still be closed manually. Opening UIB/UOB does not by itself prove the UPS DC section is safe or de-energised.
